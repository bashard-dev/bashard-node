# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unsolicited private contact and
publishing credentials are not acceptable. Respect privacy and disclose
security issues privately. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and
limit participation to protect the community. Report concerns to
support@bashard.com; include only information necessary to evaluate the report.
Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and avoid promises of confidentiality
they cannot guarantee.
